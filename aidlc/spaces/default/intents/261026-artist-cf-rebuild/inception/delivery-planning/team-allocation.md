# Team Allocation

## Who Builds Each Bolt

A **mob** is the group that builds a Bolt together; a **Bolt** is one build pass over a piece of the work, ending in something that runs. This project is solo (`inception/practices-discovery/team-practices.md`), and Team Formation was not part of the `mvp` plan. So every Bolt is built by the AI developer, with the owner approving each checkpoint. Bolts and units are as in `bolt-plan.md` and `inception/units-generation/unit-of-work.md`.

| Bolt | Unit | Built by | Approved by |
|------|------|----------|-------------|
| Bolt 1 | u1-skeleton | AI developer (aidlc-developer-agent) | Owner |
| Bolt 2 | u2-pipeline | AI developer (aidlc-developer-agent) | Owner |
| Bolt 3 | u3-web | AI developer (aidlc-developer-agent) | Owner |

## Owner Tasks

The owner also runs the full-size pipeline on their own machine in Bolt 2 and does the by-eye quality check in Bolt 3.
